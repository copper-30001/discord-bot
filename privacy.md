# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 14 November 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot (the "Bot", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when users interact with its services.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the Bot may collect or process the following information:

- **Discord identifiers:** User IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs and message IDs.
- **User information:** Discord usernames, display names and publicly available profile information.
- **Message content:** Messages, commands and other content submitted to or processed by the Bot where necessary for its functionality.
- **Interaction data:** Commands, button interactions, configuration settings and feature preferences.
- **Technical information:** Error logs, timestamps and operational information required for troubleshooting and service maintenance.

The Bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless necessary for a clearly identified feature.

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected information may be used to:

- Provide and maintain the Bot's functionality.
- Process commands and respond to user interactions.
- Store server-specific settings and user preferences.
- Detect abuse, prevent misuse and maintain security.
- Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be processed temporarily or stored where necessary to operate the Bot.

- Temporary information may be discarded after processing.
- Configuration settings may be retained while the Bot remains installed in a server.
- Operational logs may be retained for troubleshooting and security purposes.
- Stored information will be deleted or anonymised when no longer reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, unless retention is required by law.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the functionality involved.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates using Discord's platform and may interact with external APIs, hosting providers or other services to deliver its features.

Where third-party services are used, relevant information may be transmitted to those providers solely as necessary to perform the requested functionality.

Third-party services may process information under their own privacy policies.

We take reasonable steps to limit the information shared with external services.

## 6. Artificial Intelligence Features

Where the Bot provides AI-powered functionality, user-submitted content may be processed by automated systems to generate responses or perform requested tasks.

AI processing may take place on infrastructure operated by us or through third-party AI service providers.

We do not intentionally use personal information to train AI models without appropriate notice or a lawful basis.

Users should avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information unless necessary and appropriate for the intended functionality.

## 7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, users may request:

- Access to personal information held about them.
- Correction of inaccurate personal information.
- Deletion of personal information where applicable.
- Information about how their personal data is processed.

Requests may be subject to reasonable identity verification and applicable legal limitations.

## 9. Data Deletion

Users may stop interacting with the Bot at any time.

Server administrators may remove the Bot from their Discord servers.

Removing the Bot does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of all previously stored information.

Users may request deletion of their personal information through the contact method specified in Section 12. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable laws.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to address the matter.

## 11. International Data Processing

Information may be processed or stored in locations outside Hong Kong, depending on the hosting infrastructure and third-party services used.

Where applicable, we will take reasonable steps to protect personal information in accordance with relevant data protection requirements.

## 12. Contact

For privacy enquiries, data access requests, corrections or deletion requests, please contact the Bot operator through the support or contact channel provided on the Bot's official Discord application profile.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in the Bot's functionality, data practices or applicable legal requirements.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
